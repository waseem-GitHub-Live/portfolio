About Me

Three years deep in Android, one app at a time.

I'm a Senior Android Developer based in Faisalabad, Pakistan, currently leading development at Ainsoft Tech. My work centers on media-heavy apps — video players built on FFmpeg and MPV, video editors on Media3/ExoPlayer, and document scanners powered by ML Kit, OpenCV, and iText.

I manage the full app lifecycle: architecture decisions, modular MVVM/Clean Architecture setups with Hilt and Coroutines, monetization through AdMob and in-app purchases, and the final push to the Play Store. I care about apps that feel fast and stay maintainable long after launch.
